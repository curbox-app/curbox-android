NEW:
• Curbox now has a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox.
• Use the curbox sync app to sync usage and focus mode between multiple devices.
• Urge Surfing: tap "I'm struggling" on a block screen to ride out a craving. Curbox also offers it after 3 unlocks in a row.
• Sip & Stretch: take short breaks to drink water and move. Apps stay blocked during the break.
• Scheduled Notifications: get your notifications together at set times, not all day long.
• Advanced analytics with a usage heatmap.
• App groups can now add apps you install later on their own.
• Focus Mode plays a sound when a session, Pomodoro, or break starts and ends.
• Added browser support for /e/OS Browser and Waterfox.
• API: exact focus end times, group ids, usage history, and focus listeners.

IMPROVEMENTS:
• Curbox is now a smaller download. (8mb to 3mb)
• The Instagram reel and Yt counter is more accurate and no longer counts reels you skip past in a fast swipe.
• Mindful messages stay hidden while your phone is locked.
• API access ends when the connected app is uninstalled.
• Updated the Vietnamese translation.

FIXES:
• Fixed anti uninstall not working when the phone uses another language.
• Fixed groups that stayed off after "temporarily turn off" ran out.
• Fixed app unlocks and session timers resetting when another window appeared.
• Fixed editing a Focus Mode group creating a new one.
• Fixed grayscale bugs.
• Fixed the brightness slider crash in the color picker.
